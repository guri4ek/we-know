# Stage-1 answer-form examples

Date: 2026-10-11. The completed [humor research](../research/humor-mechanisms.md) and [guide](answer-generation-guide.md) were read before these examples were written. Their files and drafts were preserved during stage 1; the later documentation audit translated the prose without regenerating examples. Below are new author illustrations of [taxonomy v1](taxonomy-v1.md), **not a catalog, seed, or approved eligibility**. Human scores are `null`; every candidate is `hold` pending human editing and broader probes. Jev does not receive them.

Each row describes only a proposed `(intent, GENERAL)` pair and one primary `ANSWER_ELIGIBILITY.humor_mechanism`. The mechanism is not an ANSWER field and does not influence future uniform random selection. This is a text document, not a new import/schema contract.

| Intent | Draft answer | Primary mechanism | Three different fit probes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YES_NO | Yes. The objection has been promoted to supporting evidence. | reversal | Is Monday compulsory? / Can a calendar hold a grudge? / Is my browser in charge? |
| WHEN | After the calendar receives permission to proceed. | bureaucratic_absurdity | When will the update arrive? / When will the meeting end? / When will adulthood begin? |
| WHY | Because the explanation submitted its conclusion before its evidence. | false_causality | Why are meetings endless? / Why do apps need accounts? / Why is progress slow? |
| HOW | Appoint a supervisor for the first step. The remaining steps will request supervisors. | bureaucratic_absurdity | How do I organize a project? / How do I learn a tool? / How do I establish a routine? |
| WHO | The calendar. It has already assigned everyone else a supporting role. | reversal | Who invented meetings? / Who owns Monday? / Who runs the office? |
| WHAT | A subscription to the possibility of completion. Cancellation requires completion. | metaphor | What is productivity? / What is progress? / What is adulthood? |
| HOW_MANY | One infinity, rounded up to the nearest committee. | hyperbole | How many meetings are enough? / How many tabs do I need? / How many hobbies count as a personality? |
| CHOICE | The option with less paperwork. Indecision has already opened a second office. | bureaucratic_absurdity | Tea or coffee? / A notebook or a spreadsheet? / Morning or afternoon meetings? |
| OTHER | The conclusion is ready. A suitable question will be recruited shortly. | reversal | Monday again. / Another meeting appeared. / My browser has ambitions. |
| ANY | Certainty has been issued. Please retain the doubt as proof of purchase. | metaphor | When will this end? / Who invented Monday? / Tea or coffee? |

The universal draft is only `(ANY, GENERAL)`. Losing the answer form at this tier is future explicit degradation; OTHER remains an ordinary classified form.

**Known negative probes, not executable exclusions:** the WHEN phrase does not supply duration for `How long will this last?`; the WHO phrase does not fit `Who should I marry?`; the WHAT phrase does not fit `What should I cook?`. YES_NO and HOW_MANY need separate screening on HEALTH/MONEY, where the form can sound like real advice. Positive probes therefore do not prove whole-pool eligibility. Do not import these broad GENERAL eligibilities without repairing/rejecting them; do not introduce runtime filters, new labels, or mechanism weights to rescue examples.

No independent evaluation of fit, humor, or repetition fatigue has occurred. Production content and import belong to a separate stage; this document illustrates form and evidence gaps only.
